Cookie Policy

What cookies are, which ones Voile Kingdom uses, and how you can control them.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Voile Kingdom uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit www.voilekingdom.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences, such as whether you have already dismissed a notice, and give website owners information about how their pages are used. Cookies can be “first-party” (set by us) or “third-party” (set by another service we use).

The cookies we use

Essential cookies

These are needed for the website to work properly, for example to remember your cookie choice and to keep forms secure. They do not identify you personally and cannot be switched off in our systems.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are most popular and how people move between them. The information is collected in aggregate form and helps us improve the site. Common Google Analytics cookies include _ga and _ga_*, which typically last up to two years.

Advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow ads to be more relevant to you and can limit how often you see the same ad. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Managing your cookie preferences

When you first visit our website, a notice lets you accept all cookies or keep essential cookies only. You can also control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or delete cookies when you close the browser. Please note that blocking some cookies may affect how the website works.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we change the tools we use or if regulations change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
