The collections

The heart of the house

Dresses

Our dresses are cut to move. Most have a relaxed body, a defined but unfussy waist and a length that works with flat sandals as easily as with a heel. You will find breezy linen shirt dresses, tiered cotton voile maxis and a few silk slips for evenings.

  • Midi and maxi lengths, with a few knee-length day dresses
  • Pockets in almost every style
  • Lined bodices on sheer fabrics
  • Sizes XS to XL, with measurements listed for each piece

For sun, sand and slow days

Resortwear

Resortwear is where we have the most fun. Loose kaftans with hand-finished embroidery, printed cover-ups that go from beach to lunch, and matching sets that pack flat and come out of the suitcase looking fine.

  • Kaftans and long cover-ups
  • Co-ord shirt and trouser sets
  • Prints designed in our studio
  • Lightweight fabrics that dry quickly

The pieces that tie it together

Separates

Good separates are what make a small wardrobe feel big. Our linen shirts, wide-leg trousers and lightweight outer layers are planned in a shared colour palette so almost everything goes with everything else.

  • Relaxed linen and cotton shirts
  • Wide-leg and cropped trousers with elasticated backs
  • Lightweight trench coats and unlined blazers
  • Fine knit cardigans and shells

Small things, big difference

Accessories

A handful of accessories chosen to finish an outfit without overpowering it: woven straw bags, silk and cotton scarves, simple gold-tone jewellery and comfortable sandals for long walking days.

  • Handwoven straw totes and baskets
  • Large silk and cotton scarves
  • Minimal gold-tone chains and earrings
  • Strappy sandals with cushioned footbeds
