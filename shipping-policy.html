Shipping Policy

Where we ship, how long it takes and what it costs. Everything you need to know about getting your order.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

We want your order to reach you quickly and in perfect condition. This page explains how we process and ship orders placed on www.voilekingdom.com.

Order processing

Orders are packed at our New York studio and usually dispatched within 1 to 2 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays). During new collection launches or busy seasonal periods, processing may take a little longer. You will receive an email confirmation when your order is placed and another with tracking details once it ships.

Shipping within the United States

  • Standard shipping: 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. Free on orders over $150; otherwise a flat rate shown at checkout.
  • Express shipping: 1 to 3 business days after dispatch, charged at the rate shown at checkout.

We ship to all 50 states, including P.O. boxes for standard delivery. Delivery times to Alaska and Hawaii may be slightly longer.

International shipping

We ship to selected countries. Available destinations, costs and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout before you pay. International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes or customs fees charged by the destination country; these are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our prices.

Tracking your order

Every order includes tracking. Use the link in your shipping confirmation email to follow your parcel. If tracking has not updated for several days, please contact us and we will look into it with the carrier.

Delayed, lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, or has not arrived within 10 business days of the estimated delivery date, please get in touch with your order number. We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue and, where appropriate, send a replacement or issue a refund.

Incorrect addresses

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us as soon as possible; we can update the address only if the order has not yet been dispatched. We are not responsible for parcels delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer.
