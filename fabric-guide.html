Fabric & size guide

How our fabrics feel, how to care for them and how our pieces are cut. Everything we would want to know before buying something ourselves.

How to measure yourself

  • Bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level under your arms.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
  • Hip: stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

Use a soft tape measure and keep it snug but not tight. It helps to measure over light underwear rather than clothing.

Part three

Understanding our fits

Relaxed

Cut with plenty of ease through the body. Skims rather than follows your shape. Most of our linen dresses and shirts fall here.

Easy fitted

Closer through the bodice or waist, then released into a fuller skirt. Our poplin sundresses and wrap styles use this fit.

Bias cut

Fabric cut on the diagonal so it drapes and follows the body gently. Used for silk slips and skirts. It has natural give, so size down if unsure.

Part four

Care habits that make clothes last

Natural fabrics reward a little care. These are the habits we follow at home:

  • Turn garments inside out before washing to protect colour and surface texture.
  • Wash similar colours together, especially in the first few washes of linen.
  • Use a gentle, pH-neutral detergent for silk and knits. Avoid bleach and fabric softener on linen.
  • Dry in the shade. Strong sun can fade natural dyes over time.
  • Steam rather than iron silk and voile. For linen, iron while slightly damp.
  • Fold knits in a drawer so they don’t stretch on the hanger.
