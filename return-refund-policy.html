Returns & Refund Policy

Changed your mind or need a different size? Here’s how returns, exchanges and refunds work at Voile Kingdom.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

We want you to love what you buy. If something isn’t right, you can return it within 30 days of delivery, as explained below.

Return eligibility

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with all tags attached.
  • Items must be free of make-up, perfume, deodorant marks and pet hair.

Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene reasons, earrings and swimwear with a removed or damaged hygiene liner cannot be returned unless faulty. Gift cards and items marked as final sale are also non-returnable. This does not affect your rights if an item arrives faulty or not as described.

How to start a return

  1. Email info@voilekingdom.com with your order number and the item(s) you would like to return.
  2. We will reply with return instructions and the return address.
  3. Pack the items securely, include your order number, and send them using a tracked service.

Return shipping costs are the customer’s responsibility unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item. We recommend keeping your proof of postage until your refund is processed.

Refunds

Once your return arrives, we will inspect it and email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take a further few days for the funds to appear. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was faulty or incorrect.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or colour, the quickest way is to return the original item for a refund and place a new order. Because we make pieces in small runs, this helps you secure the size you want before it sells out.

Faulty or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, please contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund, including shipping costs.
